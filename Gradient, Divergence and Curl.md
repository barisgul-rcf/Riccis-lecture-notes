The **gradient** of a scalar-valued function f(x,y,z) is the vector field
$$\begin{equation*}
\text{grad}\,f=\nabla f
= \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\hat{\mathbf{i}}
+\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\hat{\mathbf{j}}
+\frac{\partial f}{\partial z} \hat{\mathbf{k}}
\end{equation*}$$
